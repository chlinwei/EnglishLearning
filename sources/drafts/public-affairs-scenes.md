# 听懂英语新闻：50个场景，500段短对话

原创主题训练文本，每段4轮中英问答，共2000轮。无音频、无时间轴、未接入首页。

所有背景均取自2025年来源；不是50条独立事件报道，也不是媒体原话。各场景围绕背景练习不同概念，假设性问题不代表事件已发生。
结构采用可复用新闻提问框架，术语、解释、误解与核查方向按主题区分；属于第一轮草稿，尚需人工去重复、口语与新闻密度评审。
真实YouTube视频尚未绑定；不得把文本数量视为真实新闻听力验收。

分配：美国15场、日本10场、韩国10场、欧洲15场。

## PA01 · 第二任期：目标不是结果

背景来源日期：2025-01-20。
事实背景：特朗普开始总统第二任期，并在就职演说中提出贸易、边境政策和物价方面的目标。
来源：https://www.whitehouse.gov/remarks/2025/01/the-inaugural-address/
重点术语：**policy agenda**（政策议程）。

### PA01-01 · 先确定报道角度

**Host:** What are we focusing on in this report?
这段报道主要关注什么？

**Reporter:** The focus is a second term, not a completed agenda. We're using the 2025 background, not presenting this as breaking news.
重点是第二任期：目标不是结果。我们使用2025年的背景，不把它当成突发新闻。

**Host:** So the event and the angle aren't quite the same thing?
所以事件和报道角度不是完全相同的东西？

**Reporter:** Right. The event gives us context; the angle tells us what question we're examining.
对。事件提供背景，角度说明我们正在研究什么问题。

### PA01-02 · 事实与官方说法

**Host:** What can we safely say about the background?
关于背景，我们可以确切说什么？

**Reporter:** Trump began his second presidential term and outlined goals on trade, border policy, and prices in his inaugural address.
特朗普开始总统第二任期，并在就职演说中提出贸易、边境政策和物价方面的目标。

**Host:** Does the official document prove that every stated goal was achieved?
官方文件能证明每个提出的目标都实现了吗？

**Reporter:** No. It records the announcement or stated position. We'd need separate evidence for the results.
不能。它记录公告或公开立场。结果需要另外的证据。

### PA01-03 · 听清关键术语

**Host:** When a reporter says policy agenda, what should I understand?
记者说policy agenda时，我应该理解为什么？

**Reporter:** An agenda sets out priorities. It doesn't prove that those priorities have been delivered.
议程列出优先事项，但不能证明这些事项已经完成。

**Host:** Could you put that in context rather than just give me a dictionary translation?
你能结合语境解释，而不只是给出词典翻译吗？

**Reporter:** In this discussion of a second term, not a completed agenda, that distinction is what keeps the meaning precise.
在第二任期：目标不是结果的讨论中，正是这个区别保证理解准确。

### PA01-04 · 不要漏掉限定条件

**Host:** What's the main qualification listeners should catch?
听众应该听清的主要限定条件是什么？

**Reporter:** A confident speech can make a proposal sound like a settled outcome.
自信的演说可能让提议听起来像既定结果。

**Host:** So a confident headline doesn't remove that qualification?
所以一个肯定的标题不能消除这个限定条件？

**Reporter:** Exactly. Listen for words such as may, could, and subject to. Don't turn a possibility into a certainty.
没错。留意may、could和subject to等词，不要把可能性变成确定性。

### PA01-05 · 记者追问证据

**Host:** What would you check before drawing a conclusion?
下结论之前你会核查什么？

**Reporter:** Compare the speech with dated policy documents and implementation records.
把演说与有日期的政策文件和执行记录进行比较。

**Host:** Would one interview be enough?
一次采访够吗？

**Reporter:** It could illustrate a person's experience, but it wouldn't establish the overall effect on its own.
它可以说明一个人的经历，但不能单独证明整体影响。

### PA01-06 · 解释不是预测

**Host:** How should a reporter explain policy agenda without overstating it?
记者如何解释policy agenda而不过度断言？

**Reporter:** An agenda sets out priorities. It doesn't prove that those priorities have been delivered.
议程列出优先事项，但不能证明这些事项已经完成。

**Host:** And what should the report avoid implying?
报道应避免暗示什么？

**Reporter:** A confident speech can make a proposal sound like a settled outcome.
自信的演说可能让提议听起来像既定结果。

### PA01-07 · 采访中的自然异议

**Host:** I see why the headline sounds clear. Aren't we making this too complicated?
我理解为什么标题听起来清楚。我们是不是把事情弄得太复杂了？

**Reporter:** I see your point, but here's the issue: a confident speech can make a proposal sound like a settled outcome.
我理解你的意思，但问题在于：自信的演说可能让提议听起来像既定结果。

**Host:** Fair enough. What would help settle the question?
有道理。什么能帮助厘清问题？

**Reporter:** Compare the speech with dated policy documents and implementation records.
把演说与有日期的政策文件和执行记录进行比较。

### PA01-08 · 区分来源与判断

**Host:** How do we tell the source's claim from the reporter's assessment?
如何分清来源的说法和记者的评估？

**Reporter:** Listen for attribution, then check the basis for the assessment. For this topic: Compare the speech with dated policy documents and implementation records.
先听来源标注，再核查评估依据。针对这个话题：把演说与有日期的政策文件和执行记录进行比较。

**Host:** So according to doesn't mean independently verified?
所以according to并不意味着已独立核实？

**Reporter:** Correct. It identifies a source. The evidence still needs to support the claim.
对。它标明来源。说法仍需证据支持。

### PA01-09 · 听力核查与纠错

**Host:** Let me check I've got this right. How should I understand policy agenda?
让我确认是否听对了。我该如何理解policy agenda？

**Reporter:** An agenda sets out priorities. It doesn't prove that those priorities have been delivered.
议程列出优先事项，但不能证明这些事项已经完成。

**Host:** And what's the mistake to watch out for?
需要避免什么误解？

**Reporter:** A confident speech can make a proposal sound like a settled outcome.
自信的演说可能让提议听起来像既定结果。

### PA01-10 · 复述与下一步

**Host:** How would you sum up the report without going beyond the evidence?
如何在不超出证据的前提下概括报道？

**Reporter:** The topic is a second term, not a completed agenda. The key explanation is this: An agenda sets out priorities. It doesn't prove that those priorities have been delivered.
